TillNext: Paycheck Planner Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2, 2026

TillNext: Paycheck Planner is a local-first paycheck planning app. It helps you organize income, expenses, bill status, savings plans, and month-to-month cash flow on your device.

For privacy questions, the controller contact for TillNext support communications is support@tillnext.app.

Data TillNext Stores On Your Device

TillNext may store the following information locally on your device when you enter it:

  • Income names, amounts, pay dates, and recurrence settings
  • Expense names, amounts, due dates, autopay flags, and recurrence settings
  • One-time expenses, future expenses, savings plans, and status changes
  • Month planning details, carryover preferences, display preferences, and setup progress
  • Terms agreement status and the local trial start date
  • App lock settings and a secure verifier for a backup PIN if you create one

This information is used to run the app's planning features.

No Account Or External Connectors

TillNext does not use accounts, external connectors, Google integrations, or any third-party account login.

No Tracking Or Analytics

TillNext does not track you across apps or websites. TillNext does not include advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or third-party tracking services.

No Data Collection By TillNext

TillNext does not send your planning data to the developer or to a developer-operated server. Your budgeting and paycheck details stay on your device unless you choose to export or share them.

Purchases

TillNext uses Apple's StoreKit for the one-time lifetime unlock purchase. Apple processes App Store purchases. TillNext checks the local App Store purchase entitlement so it can unlock editing after purchase. TillNext does not receive your payment card information.

Exports

TillNext can export a spreadsheet-friendly CSV file from Settings. If you export or share that file, the destination you choose may handle the exported data according to its own privacy practices.

Local Reset

You can remove TillNext planning data from this device by using the local reset option in Settings. This reset is scoped to TillNext data on the device.

Support Requests

If you email support, your email address, message contents, and any information you choose to include are used to respond to the request. TillNext does not attach planning data automatically. Please do not include sensitive financial details in support messages.

Support communications may be handled through email and issue-reporting providers. Depending on the tool you choose, those communications may be processed outside your country or region.

EU, EEA, And UK Privacy Rights

TillNext is designed so your planning data is stored and controlled on your device. Because the developer does not collect that in-app planning data, most privacy requests can be handled directly in the app: export a CSV to access a copy, edit entries to correct them, or use local reset/uninstall to delete TillNext data from the device.

If you contact support, you can request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or portability for the support information you sent. You may also object to processing or withdraw consent where consent is the basis for a specific support communication. To make a request, email support@tillnext.app.

You may have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Legal Bases

Local on-device processing is used to provide the app features you choose to use. Where GDPR or UK GDPR applies to support communications, the legal bases may include responding to your request, complying with legal obligations, and the developer's legitimate interests in maintaining support records and protecting the service. StoreKit purchase processing is handled by Apple under Apple's own privacy practices.

Retention

Local TillNext data stays on your device until you edit it, reset it, or remove the app. Support emails are kept only as long as needed to respond, maintain reasonable records, comply with legal obligations, and protect the service.

No Sale, Tracking, Or Automated Decisions

TillNext does not sell personal data, use third-party tracking, or make automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects.

Children's Privacy

TillNext is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

This policy may be updated when TillNext changes how it handles data. The latest version will be posted at this URL.

Contact

For support or privacy questions, email support@tillnext.app or use the TillNext support page.

TillNext stores planning data locally on your device.

Four familiar tabs. The details you need, without a wall of numbers.

Know what is available

Available to spend separates spending money from what is held for planned bills. Follow the Cashflow forecast, monthly income and expenses, and projected month-end balance before reviewing upcoming items.

Keep recurring things recurring

Add a paycheck or bill once. Review its occurrences in each month, and change one occurrence when real life changes the plan.

Plan beyond this month

Move between months and use This month to return to the current plan. Review and update an existing paycheck when it arrives early.

No account, bank connection, analytics, or tracking. Enter what you need and keep control of your planning data.

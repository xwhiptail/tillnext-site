TillNext: Paycheck Planner

Know what you have, till next paycheck.

TillNext is a local-first iPhone app for planning income, recurring bills, one-time expenses, savings, and the month ahead without connecting a bank account.

Plan Between Paychecks

See what is coming in, what needs to go out, and what is left for the month.

Keep Recurring Items Together

Add paychecks, bills, subscriptions, and other repeating items once, then review them by month.

Handle One-Time Expenses

Add repairs, gifts, fees, groceries, or other one-off spending without rebuilding a spreadsheet.

Privacy First

Your planning data stays on your iPhone.

TillNext does not use developer-operated accounts, external bank connectors, advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or third-party tracking. You can export a CSV or reset local data from Settings.

How can we help?

For setup and everyday tasks, start with the TillNext guide.

Email support

Contact support@tillnext.app for private support, purchase questions, or privacy requests.

For a bug report, include your iPhone model, iOS version, TillNext version, and the steps that led to the problem. Hide account details and personal amounts from any screenshots you send.

Report a technical issue

You can also use GitHub Issues. These reports are public, so keep personal and financial details out of them.

Quick answers

Where are my backups?

Open Settings in TillNext to export a CSV, save a manual backup, or manage iCloud Drive backups. You choose where exported files are saved.

How do I restore a supporter purchase?

In version 2.0, use Settings → Support TillNext → Restore Purchases. Use the Apple Account that made the original purchase. Earlier lifetime purchases include the supporter colors.

Do I need to pay to use version 2.0?

No. All planning features are free. One-time support is optional and adds accent colors. See pricing.

How do I remove my data?

Use the local reset action in Settings to remove planning data from this device. Keep a backup first if you may need it later. Manage exported files and iCloud Drive backups separately in Files.

TillNext: Paycheck Planner Support

TillNext: Paycheck Planner is a paycheck planning app for iPhone. It helps you see what is coming in, what needs to go out, and what is left for the month.

Get Help

For setup steps, common tasks, and screenshots, visit Help & FAQ.

Common Questions

Does TillNext require an account?

No. TillNext does not require an account or sign-in.

Does TillNext use accounts or external connectors?

No. TillNext does not use accounts, external connectors, Google integrations, ads, analytics, or tracking.

What devices does TillNext support?

TillNext is built for iPhone and requires iOS 17 or later.

Where is my data stored?

Your planning data is stored locally on your device. TillNext does not send your paycheck or budgeting details to a developer-operated server.

How do I export my data?

Open Settings in TillNext and use the CSV export action to save a spreadsheet-friendly copy.

How do I see recurring bills that start in a future month?

Open the Recurring tab and use the month control at the bottom of the screen to move to the future month. When you open a recurring item, TillNext shows the occurrence date for the month you are viewing.

How do I review past occurrences of a recurring item?

Open the Recurring tab and tap the clock button next to a recurring income or expense item. TillNext shows earlier occurrences for the same recurring rule.

How do I reset the app?

Open Settings in TillNext and use the local reset action. This removes TillNext planning data from the device.

How do I make a privacy request?

Most planning data stays on your device, so you can export it, edit it, or remove it with local reset. For support email privacy requests, including EU, EEA, and UK requests about support communications, contact support@tillnext.app.

How does the trial work?

TillNext includes a 30-day full-editing trial. After the trial ends, the app becomes read-only until you buy the one-time lifetime unlock through the App Store.

How do I restore a purchase?

Use the restore purchase action on the TillNext unlock screen. Purchases are handled by Apple's App Store.

Choose Settings → Support TillNext in the app to become a supporter.

Already bought the lifetime unlock?

Your purchase includes the supporter colors. Use Restore Purchases in Settings with the Apple Account used for your original purchase.

Do I need to pay to keep editing?

No. In version 2.1, creating, editing, exporting, and restoring your plan are free, including after an earlier trial has ended.

Does supporting change the money calculations?

No. Everyone uses the same planning features and calculations. Supporter colors only change the appearance.

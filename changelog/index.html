TillNext: Paycheck Planner Changelog

Public notes for meaningful TillNext changes. This page focuses on user-facing updates, privacy posture, and support information.

Version 1.0, Pre-Launch

Updated: May 1, 2026

  • Fixed the Home Screen widget so its Till Next amount matches Home and its colors follow the iPhone's Light or Dark Mode.
  • TillNext now follows the iPhone system appearance for Light and Dark Mode, and the separate Dark Mode setting has been removed.
  • Added a Till Next Home Screen widget with quick buttons for one-time income and expenses.
  • Updated the Till Next widget to look more like the Home screen, including the same calm card style and positive, neutral, or warning balance color.
  • Added display settings for the month picker position and planning settings for hiding Match Bank.
  • Added iCloud backup setup and local notification support.
  • Set the first public App Store release candidate to version 1.0.
  • Confirmed the public App Store name as TillNext: Paycheck Planner, with the shorter TillNext name kept for the iPhone Home Screen.
  • Set the final App Store bundle identifier to com.whiptail.TillNext before developer account enrollment.
  • Configured the official TillNext support email at support@tillnext.app.
  • Confirmed v1 device support as iPhone with iOS 17 or later.
  • Changed the first-run Terms & Privacy gate to a short consent summary with the full terms still one tap away.
  • Added a public Help & FAQ page with screenshots and common tasks.
  • Added a public support page, privacy policy, and changelog for App Store review and customer reference.
  • Added in-app support links for private email support and public technical issue reports.
  • Kept TillNext local-first with no account, no external connectors, no Google integrations, no ads, and no analytics tracking.
  • Added Terms & Privacy review during first launch and as a small link at the bottom of Settings.
  • Added a local 30-day full-editing trial followed by a one-time lifetime unlock through the App Store.
  • Added Recurring tab month navigation so future-starting recurring items can be viewed before their month arrives.
  • Added past occurrence history for recurring income and recurring expenses.
  • Improved recurring item details so opening an item from another month shows that month's occurrence date.
  • Improved recurring expense rows so they show the selected month's expense days next to the cadence, such as Monthly • 25 or Twice a month • 1 & 15.
  • Added a Today shortcut beside income and expense date pickers for faster one-time and recurring entry.
  • When saving same-day one-time income or expenses, TillNext now offers to keep the item Pending or mark it Done/Paid.
  • Preserved CSV export and local reset from Settings.

Support

For private support, email support@tillnext.app.

For technical bug reports, use GitHub Issues.

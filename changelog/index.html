TillNext: Paycheck Planner Changelog

Public notes for meaningful TillNext changes. This page focuses on user-facing updates, privacy posture, and support information.

Version 1.1.9

Updated: August 5, 2026

August 5, 2026

  • Rebuilt TillNext around a deliberate Tilly's Quest HUD, with sharper day and night panels, compact stat displays, divider-based lists, and clearer visual priority for Safe cash.
  • Docked the optional Tilly guide above the tab rail, removed automatic chatter and dragging, and made one tap open a focused planning panel.
  • Updated the optional Tilly guide to use Tilly's canonical Tilly's Quest pixel sprite.
  • Improved Safe cash so a saved bank check can roll forward across months as income and bills are marked done, reducing the need to re-enter Cash today.
  • Replaced the daily stale-cash warning with a clearer note when Safe cash is being estimated from the last bank check.

The fine print, in plain words

PRIVACY POLICY

Effective June 14, 2026

The short version █

Tilly's Quest does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal information. There are no accounts and no ads. Your game progress is saved only on your own device. That's really it.

1. Who this covers

This policy applies to the Tilly's Quest mobile app ("the app", "we", "us") published by Whiptail. By using the app you agree to the practices described here.

2. Information we collect

None. The app does not ask for, collect, or store any personally identifiable information. We don't request your name, email, location, contacts, photos, or any account sign-in. We have no servers that receive data from the app.

3. Data stored on your device

To make the game playable, the app saves your progress locally on your device using the operating system's standard on-device storage. This includes things like:

  • Your character's level, experience, and in-game coins
  • Story progress, unlocked areas, and opened treasure
  • Settings such as whether audio is muted

This information never leaves your device. It is not transmitted to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app removes this data.

4. No tracking, analytics, or advertising

The app contains no third-party analytics, no advertising networks, no trackers, and no marketing SDKs. We do not build a profile of you and there is nothing to opt out of.

5. No network connection required

Tilly's Quest is designed to run entirely offline. It does not require an internet connection and does not send your information over a network.

6. Children's privacy

Tilly's Quest is family-friendly and suitable for children. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children. It contains no ads, no in-app purchases, and no links that lead children away from the game.

7. Device permissions

The app plays music and sound effects through your device's standard audio output. It does not access your microphone, camera, location, contacts, or photo library.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, we'll update this page and revise the effective date above. Material changes will be reflected here before they take effect.

9. Contact

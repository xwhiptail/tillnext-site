Tilly's Quest Changelog

Public notes for meaningful Tilly's Quest changes. This page focuses on user-facing updates, privacy posture, and support information.

Version 1.1 — Upcoming

Status: In development, July 2026

  • Added the optional Investing Expansion (one-time purchase, behind a parental gate): the Market Beyond realm — Stock Exchange, Bond Vault, and Crypto Caverns — with the Bull Baron, Coupon King, Crypto Bro, and Compounder bosses (and one very well-hidden auditor).
  • Expansion extras: Tilly outfit skins, twelve discoverable ability Glyphs, the Portfolio Run minigame, heist minigames, and Gordon the Quant's augment forge. See The Market Beyond.
  • Added investing questions to the boss quiz pool.
  • Added a one-tap Feedback & Suggestions box on the title screen.
  • Kept the game free of ads, analytics, tracking, and accounts.

Version 1.0

Updated: June 2026

  • Initial release of Tilly's Quest as its own app, grown from the mini-game inside TillNext: Paycheck Planner.
  • Full story: battle bill monsters across Paycheck Plains, the underworld, and heaven, and face Uncle Sam's Final Audit.
  • Turn-based battles with money-themed abilities, level-up upgrades, and mid-boss finance quizzes.
  • Shops, treasure chests, secrets, riddlers, lore papers, and skill minigames.
  • Three save slots, New Game+, and the once-a-day Daily Gauntlet with optional Game Center leaderboards.
  • iCloud save backup to the player's own account, Auto-Battle and Turbo accessibility options, and a full original soundtrack.
  • No ads, analytics, tracking, or accounts.

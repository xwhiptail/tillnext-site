Tilly's Quest Support

Tilly's Quest is a pixel-art RPG for iPhone and iPad that teaches money concepts through battles and minigames.

Get Help

For gameplay questions, saves, and purchases, visit Help & FAQ.

For private support, email support@tillnext.app, or use the Feedback & Suggestions box on the game's title screen.

For technical bug reports, you can also open a public GitHub Issue:

https://github.com/xwhiptail/tillnext-site/issues

Common Questions

Does Tilly's Quest require an account?

No. There are no accounts, sign-ins, ads, analytics, or tracking.

What devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad (portrait), iOS 17 or later. The game plays fully offline.

Where is my progress stored?

On your device, with an optional automatic backup to your own iCloud account so progress survives a new phone. The developer cannot access your saves.

How do I restore the Investing Expansion on a new device?

Open the expansion screen in the game and tap Restore Purchases. Purchases are handled by Apple and tied to the Apple ID that bought them.

What is the parental gate?

Before any purchase can begin, the game asks a grown-up to type a spelled-out number. It keeps purchases in adult hands.

How do I reset my game?

From the title screen's slot chooser, delete the save slot you want to clear. Deleting a slot also removes its iCloud backup.

How do I make a privacy request?

Game data stays on your device or in your own iCloud account, so most requests can be handled by managing saves in the app. For feedback submissions or support-email requests, including EU, EEA, and UK requests, contact support@tillnext.app.

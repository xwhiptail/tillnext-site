Questions & Answers

Frequently asked

Tilly's Quest saves automatically as you play — after battles, when you pick up items, and as you move through the world. There's no save button to remember.

Open the in-game menu with the ≡ button at the top-left. From there you can start a fresh game. Finishing the quest also unlocks New Game+ for a tougher run that carries your progress forward.

There are no ads and no in-app purchases anywhere in the game. The coins you spend are part of the adventure — they're never real money.

No. Tilly's Quest plays entirely offline. It doesn't require an account, sign-in, or network connection.

No personal data is collected. Your progress is stored only on your device. See the Privacy Policy for the full details.

Check the speaker icon in the top bar to make sure audio isn't muted. Also confirm your phone's silent / ring switch is off and the volume is up. If music still won't play, fully close and reopen the app.

Saves live on the device and aren't synced to the cloud. An encrypted device-to-device transfer or full iCloud device backup will carry the save over; a fresh install on a new phone starts a new quest.

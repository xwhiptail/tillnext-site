What is Tilly's Quest?

A pixel-art RPG that quietly teaches budgeting — your money smarts are your weapons. Every battle move mirrors a real money habit.

Plan the Paycheck

Bank Plan Points each turn, then spend them on the right move. It's turn-based combat built around a budget.

Defend with Safe Cash

Block incoming bills with a savings shield, and feel why a buffer beats living payday to payday.

Pop-Quiz Bosses

Boss fights pause for a real money question — or flip on Skill Minigames and endorse checks, sort coins, and stack budgets instead. Ace it either way to strike big.

Explore & Loot

Cross plains, the underworld, and heaven; crack gear-lock chests for coins and permanent upgrades.

Who it's for

Made for anyone who wants money to feel a little less scary.

Curious kids

Friendly pixel art, short sessions, and forgiving fail states — an Emergency Fund of second chances — make first money lessons fun and pressure-free.

Money-anxious adults

Practice planning income, saving, and dodging high-interest debt — by playing, not studying.

Families

A wholesome co-play pick: learn budgeting basics together, one payday at a time.

The Daily Gauntlet

One run a day, twisted by a rotating curse — everyone in the world plays the same one.

A New Curse Every Day

Ten rotating mutators warp the rules: MOSH PIT crowds, VHS DAY static, POP QUIZ every third turn, MIMIC FRENZY chests that bite back… you get one shot at today's.

Build a Streak

Clear the gauntlet day after day to grow your streak — and your bragging rights. Miss a day and the counter resets to zero.

Game Center Leaderboards

Daily score, best streak, and lifetime coins climb real leaderboards, with achievements from your first cleared gauntlet to felling fifty bosses.

Saves That Follow You

iCloud backup carries your kingdom between iPhone and iPad, and a Records screen keeps your trophies close.

Sprite Vault

Every idle sprite in the game — the hero, the bill-monsters of all nine realms, and the bosses.

The Quest

Money skills, turned into a game

Every battle teaches a budgeting move you can use in real life.

Plan the Paycheck

Bank Plan Points each turn, then spend them on the right move. It's turn-based combat built around a budget.

Defend with Safe Cash

Block incoming bills with a savings shield. Learn why an emergency buffer beats living paycheck to paycheck.

Pop-Quiz Bosses

Big fights pause for a finance quiz from a 50-question pool — answer well and hit harder. Knowledge is damage.

Gear-Lock Chests

Crack treasure with a timing minigame, then spend coins on augments that permanently buff your budget.

Why it works

Sneaky financial literacy

Learn by doing, not reading

Budgeting ideas — planning income, building a safety buffer, avoiding high-interest debt, the cost of compounding — are baked into the moves you make every turn. You feel the concept before you have a name for it.

Made for all ages

Friendly pixel art, short sessions, and no punishing fail states make it great for curious kids and money-anxious adults alike. No purchases, no ads, no pressure.

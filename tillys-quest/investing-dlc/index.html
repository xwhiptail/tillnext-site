What's in the expansion?

The budgeting lessons graduate to investing — risk, diversification, volatility, and patience — all through play.

The Market Beyond

A new late-game realm past Uncle Sam: stroll the Stock Exchange floor, the Bond Vault, and the neon Crypto Caverns, each its own painted room and theme.

Four New Bosses

The charging Bull Baron, the armored Coupon King, the meme-slinging Crypto Bro, and the towering Compounder — the expansion's capstone.

Engraved Glyphs

Discover ability Glyphs as you clear the realm, then engrave them to re-tune Tilly's abilities — lifesteal, reflect, compounding PP, and more.

A minigame: split a stash across cash, bonds, stocks, and crypto, then weather five market-event rounds. Diversify, or get rugged.
